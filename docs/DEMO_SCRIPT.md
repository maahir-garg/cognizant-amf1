# Pitch and live demo script

Thursday 8 October 2026, Cognizant Singapore. The slot is 15 minutes including the live demo, followed by 5 minutes of questions. Judging: innovation, desirability, business opportunity and market potential, viability, ease of implementation (1 to 5 each).

Two people: the **driver** runs the laptop, the **narrator** speaks. Everything runs from the pitch laptop in offline demo mode.

Every click below was checked against the build on 1 Oct 2026. Rehearse on the final build and update this file if a label moves.

## Pre-flight (30 minutes before, on the pitch laptop)

1. From the repo: `npm run build`, then `npm start` (serves on `http://localhost:3000`). With no `.env.local`, demo mode is on and nothing calls the network.
2. Chrome, one window, full screen at 1920×1080, zoom 100%. Close every other tab.
3. Reset local state: DevTools, Application, Local storage, clear `localhost:3000`. This clears the depth choice, travel plan, quiz badge and any approvals from rehearsal.
4. Open each route once to warm it: `/`, `/weekend/singapore-2026`, `/share`, `/partners`, `/how-it-works`, `/sources`. Return to `/`.
5. Put the Check my draft line on the clipboard manager or in a text file on the desktop (see 7:00 below). Do not type it live.
6. Second window, minimised: the backup video ready to play, `docs/demo-video/off-camera-demo.mp4` once it is re-recorded on the final build (milestone M8; until then `impact-lap-demo.mp4` shows the previous build).
7. Wi-Fi off. Say so during the demo; it is part of the point.
8. Mirror the display and check the projector shows the full width.

## Likely questions

1. **Where does the data come from, and how do you know it is right?** The 2025 and 2024 Make A Mark reports, the Manifesto and the Make A Mark page, plus DEFRA and EPA factors for travel comparisons. Every verified fact has a page and a verbatim quote that an automated check re-finds on that page. Estimates are recomputed from their inputs. See `docs/data-sources.md`.
2. **Is the AI making things up?** It is handed only the facts for the request and must cite each claim. The guardrail rejects any number that does not match a cited fact. A draft that fails twice is replaced by a grounded template. The label says what drafted it.
3. **Is any of this live?** No, and we do not say it is. Figures are "Updated when the team publishes". The pilot adds one approved per-race feed with a named owner, and the cadences are set out in `docs/ROI.md`.
4. **What does Singapore look like, if per-race figures are not published?** Real programmes, a travel tip and a data gap for trackside energy. The season-average split is kept in the detail layer and never labelled as Singapore's.
5. **What would the pilot cost, and who pays?** Year one is proposed as Cognizant value-in-kind within the partnership. The ROI tab shows our planning assumption for a first-year pilot, roughly £0.42m to £0.62m, built from the pilot team in `docs/ROI.md`; it is not a team or Cognizant figure, and the data-sharing agreement would firm it up.
6. **What does Cognizant own?** The fact base pipeline, the guardrail and generation service, the desk and the API. The team owns the data, the approvals and the fan brand. See `docs/architecture.md`.
7. **How is this different from AWorld?** AWorld is a broad engagement platform with learning, challenges and motorsport work. Off Camera is narrower: one team's own report evidence, organised around the car, with a numeric check and approvals for partner content. They could sit side by side.
8. **What about the charities?** The story kit gives Make A Mark charity partners co-branded, checked paragraphs, including a funder report format, from the same facts and the same approval path.
