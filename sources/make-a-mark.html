                    RACING FOR CHANGE

                    Since Aston Martin's return to Formula One in 2021, a commitment to ESG has been at the heart of everything we do. Our success is not only measured on track but also our ability to effect positive change in our team, as well as the communities and environment around us. ﻿
                    Our work is guided by three core pillars: Environment, Belong and Community. We track and report our progress against these pillars to ensure we are making a lasting positive impact and contributing to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, focusing on where we can make the greatest and most credible difference through our operations and partnerships.

                    1. ENVIRONMENT

                    Building a more sustainable business and operation for the benefit of our planet.  

                    2. BELONG

                    Fostering a culture rooted in respect, support and inclusion, where people feel valued and able to thrive. 

                    3. COMMUNITY

                    Supporting communities to improve lives and expand opportunity for the future.  

                    MAKE A MARK IS OUR COMMITMENT TO DRIVING MEANINGFUL PROGRESS FOR PEOPLE, OUR PLANET AND OUR PARTNERS.

                    Net Zero by 2050

                    2025 marked a defining step in our sustainability journey: we reached an important milestone with the validation of our Science Based Targets, reinforcing our commitment to achieving net zero greenhouse gas emissions across our value chain by 2050.

                    Our near-term targets include a 42 per cent reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions, and a 25 per cent reduction in Scope 3 emissions by 2030. 

                    In addition to strengthening our net zero pathway, we achieved a 14 per cent reduction in travel and logistics emissions through smarter logistics planning and investment in Sustainable Aviation Fuel. A total of 1,188 tCO₂e were avoided, which is equivalent to 88,153 laps around Silverstone. 

                    We also launched our first carbon removal investment programme to support climate action, biodiversity and local communities.

                    These innovative carbon removal projects include concrete mineralisation, mangrove restoration, and community woodland programmes, removing more than 2,000 tCO₂e in 2025. These initiatives not only lock away carbon permanently but also enhance biodiversity and support local communities through sustainable livelihoods.

                    Aleto Leadership Programme

                    Building a culture where people feel valued and able to thrive remained central to our work in 2025.

                    We have continued to invest in inclusion and wellbeing, marked key moments that celebrate difference, and built practical pathways into the sport through mentoring and development programmes that support underrepresented talent.

                    The third year of our Aleto Leadership Programme saw us welcome 15 university students from underrepresented backgrounds for mentorship by members of the Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team over a nine-month period. 

                    The programme included an intensive skills development week, mock assessments and mentoring, providing participants with a holistic insight into careers and leadership development. 

                    Combining mentoring with a series of development activities and intensive placement weeks to build leadership skills, the programme helps prepare students for the F1 recruitment process, and engages them in STEM and motorsport, alongside providing support as participants transition from university into professional careers.

                    Upon completion of the programme, 93 per cent of the 2025 cohort felt they grew their professional network and 90 per cent felt they grew their leadership skills, public speaking and confidence.

                    Make A Mark week

                    Our fourth annual Make A Mark Week engaged 257 students across 14 schools and community groups in STEM and career development workshops, delivered in collaboration with key partners, including Arm, Cognizant, ELEMIS and Xerox.

                    Ahead of the British Grand Prix, we invited secondary school students and undergraduate university students to the AMR Technology Campus. Together with our partners, we ﻿provided a series of sessions to support learning and to inform young people about the opportunities available within our business and the wider STEM sector.  

                    Signal to speed

                    In this hands-on workshop, our official AI Compute Platform Partner Arm guided students on using Micro:bit’s CreateAI. Demonstrating ways of training a machine learning model to recognise Formula One-style hand gestures, the sessions combined education on coding, AI understanding and teamwork in a fast-paced engineering challenge.

                    Future ready: Exploring tomorrow's careers with tech

                    In a discussion-led session, Cognizant, our Global AI Services Partner, allowed students to explore the ways in which technology and AI are transforming industries. With a focus on identifying career opportunities, the discussion encouraged critical thinking and provided education around emerging STEM roles and the skills needed to thrive.

                    ﻿Augmenting reality: Create and explore with CareAR 

                    Xerox's session revolved around teaching the power of augmented reality. Offering hands-on experience with CareAR, Xerox taught students how to use no-code tools to create interactive solutions and develop key digital and soft skills. 

                    Race against the clock to save the world from climate change 

                    Our ﻿Official Skincare Partner, ELEMIS, set a fast-paced, collaborative card-based challenge. Encouraging students to uncover the science behind climate change, attendees had to connect cause and effect while racing to find solutions before time ran out.

                    Unearth Your Greatness with Maaden

                    2025 saw the launch of Unearth Your Greatness, a global multi-year youth development initiative developed in partnership with the team's Principal Partner, Maaden.

                    The initiative is designed to identify, develop and inspire the next generation of future industry leaders. It aims to celebrate the greatness that comes from the dedication to craft, both within the world of Formula One and the Mining Industry, and equip young people with the knowledge, skills and hands-on learning experience needed to explore STEM related careers in both industries.

                    Aligned with the Kingdom's Vision 2030 goal to modernise education and grow a knowledge-based economy, Maaden's investment will provide access to resources for both students and teachers, helping to integrate professional learning. The platform will provide greater inclusion in STEM fields to create lasting pathways from education and industry, to help drive recruitment of the best talent.

                    As part of the Unearth Your Greatness, Maaden was announced as Official Supporting Partner of STEM Racing, the global F1 supported programme where students design, build and race CO2 powered cars.

                    To support this, Maaden is providing a brand-new bespoke online learning platform – the Global STEM Racing Learning Channel – creating new flexibility for STEM Racing engagement, both for educators and learners. Interactive modules, lessons and certifications can be leveraged in classrooms or at home by students to aid self-led learning. By 2028, this platform is expected to engage 100,000 students and teachers globally.

                    Female mentorship

                    The Accelerate ARM x Aston Martin Aramco Formula One Team women's mentorship programme brought together mentors and mentees from across our industry, demonstrating the power of representation, connection and shared learning.

                    Launched during International Women’s Day 2025, the Accelerate Women Leadership and Mentorship Programme is a collaboration between Arm and Aston Martin Aramco Formula One™ Team to support the progression of women in technology and motorsport.

                    The six-month pilot paired women mentors from Arm with women mentees from Aston Martin Aramco Formula One™ Team, providing structured support across leadership development, technical confidence, career navigation and personal growth.

                    By connecting expertise across both organisations, the programme builds meaningful networks and helps strengthen the future female talent pipeline.

                    10 mentee-mentor pairs from Arm and Aston Martin Aramco participated in the programme. 

                    71 per cent of mentees surveyed rated the programme's sessions as highly valuable.

                    Many mentees reported that the sessions provided them with a sounding board for real-life challenges around leadership, confidence, career navigation and imposter syndrome. 

                    The programme reached a wide audience on social media, generating 700,000 impressions, 12,300 interactions, and 95.5 per cent positive sentiment.

                    B CDP Performance

                    CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project) is a global non-profit that helps companies report their environmental impact, including emissions, water use, and climate risks.

                    We are proud to have achieved a CDP score of B in our climate change disclosure, reflecting our commitment to transparency, climate action, and responsible environmental management.

                    This places the team above the CDP average and in the top 35 per cent of all companies disclosing through CDP.﻿

                    MANAGING OUR ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

                    We have implemented an Environmental Management System by establishing our sustainability policy, best practice standards and delivering environmental awareness training to our people. A 'Responsible Citizen, Responsible Company' culture prevails at our team: our people are empowered to take action for the betterment of the planet, and we have been awarded FIA Three Star Environmental Accreditation and certification to ISO 14001: Environmental Management Standard.

                    MEANINGFUL PROGRESS

                    ENERGY MANAGEMENT

                    We are the first F1 team to comply with ISO standard 50001 – a globally recognised certification that outlines the requirements for implementing an energy management system. Our goal for ISO 50001 is to improve energy performance, increase energy efficiency, and reduce energy consumption and associated costs.

                    LEADING THE WAY

                    TEAMING UP WITH ALETO

                    We've partnered with The Aleto Foundation to provide a bespoke leadership programme for young ethnic minority students who want to learn more about opportunities within motorsport and the world of F1 and have a keen interest in pursuing STEM careers. Designed to develop students' skills, knowledge, and networks within the motorsport industry, the nine-month educational programme includes a Leadership Masterclass Day at the AMR Technology Campus, work experience with the team, and each student is assigned a mentor from the team.
